# Current MVP

## Available in the app

- Core 300 catalog with simple names, five-category browsing and scrollable search.
- Gram-based food selection with automatically calculated calories.
- Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner and Snack meal logging.
- Water tracking in US fluid ounces, with quick-add amounts.
- Effective-dated calorie/water goals and daily progress.
- Day/week/month history and deletion of food/water entries.
- Persistent local storage, including browser storage on a stable origin.

## Supported by the repository; UI work remains

- Multi-food meals and editing saved entries.
- Protein, carbohydrate and fat calculations and totals.
- Live query streams for screens that need them.

## Remaining work

- Add meal/water editing controls and macro summaries.
- Implement settings beyond the current placeholder.
- Verify final native builds and browser deployment storage behavior.

There are no accounts or cloud sync. Weight, height, workouts and sleep are outside this MVP. See [database APIs](DATABASE_GUIDE.md), [catalog maintenance](../tool/FOOD_CLEANUP.md), and [team workflow](TEAM_WORKFLOW.md).
